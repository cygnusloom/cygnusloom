### 2026-11-06 live playtest (real DeepSeek, browser UI)

Tested `cairn2e` (Twin Lakes), `worldswithoutnumber` (Unwritten World), and `academia-arcana` (Unwritten Academy), ~12-16 player turns each. No code changes. Severity is the tester's initial read; triage as usual.

- **Cairn: player hazard saves do not roll for the player (high).** At the Deadmill the GM prompted "Roll a DEX save" and "Make a STR save"; clicking **Roll 1d20** created no player save event. The log shows `player_save` returning `{"error": "no player character owes a save"}`, after which the GM calls `roll_save` and narrates the result. `player_save` is only valid when `pc.save_due` is set (`src/cygnusloom/services/combat/save.py:132`), but the engine queues the player prompt before validating. Expected: either a general player-save tool for hazard saves, or no prompt when no save is owed; the player's Roll should produce and display the die. Reproduced twice.
- **WWN: overland travel always fails (high).** `move_actor(mode="overland", ...)` at The Grey Marrow returned `{"error": "this rule set does not track wilderness"}`. `worldswithoutnumber` has travel rules and `world_terrain.json` but no `wilderness` block; `src/cygnusloom/services/world_travel.py` requires one. Expected: overland travel/supply/weather using the WWN travel rules, or remove the overland path from WWN.
- **Academia Arcana: Year can exceed the 7-year cap (high).** Two school-year endings in one turn (one GM `end_period` plus one injected test call) produced Year 8 and 8 Advancements; the turn review passed it. `counters.json` gives `year` `min: 1` but no maximum, and `periods.json` always applies `year +1`. Rules say Years 1-7 with a +4 stat cap only in Year 7. Expected: `end_period` refuses or clamps at Year 7.
- **Academia Arcana: raising a stat at cap consumes an Advancement (high).** With Theory at 4, "Raise a stat: Theory" recorded `character.advancement_taken`, changed Advancements 4→3, and produced no `character.max_changed`. Expected: refuse or disable capped stats; do not spend the point.
- **Academia Arcana: "Take a new subject" offers the current Major (high).** Elowen's Major was Illusion; the advancement form offered "Take a new subject: Illusion" and the server accepted it, adding a duplicate Illusion feature. Expected: exclude studied subjects and the Major, with the same check server-side.
- **WWN: starting kit overfills carrying capacity, overload never applies, and 0-slot items are refused (high).** A random-created character showed "Carrying 16 of 9 slots" with no warning and no Overloaded penalty. `give_item` then refused a 0-slot item: "Stowed pool is full: 13 of 6 slots used, and Wax tablet ... takes 0." `src/cygnusloom/services/inventory.py` checks `used + item.slots > capacity` (rejecting even when `slots == 0`) and never applies the configured overload penalty for named pools. Expected: validate/warn at creation, apply Overloaded when over capacity, and allow 0-slot items.
- **WWN: attack prompts repeat outside an active turn (medium).** `player_attack` returned "no one is taking a turn..." four times, and the same attack prompt was re-issued three times before resolving. The encounter had started but no turn had been begun. Expected: start the PC turn after initiative or make the prompt/tool contract clear.
- **Academia Arcana: 7-9 "choose one" outcomes have no choice UI (medium).** Move results list the options ("draw attention / side effect / mark 1 Stress") as text but show no buttons or prompt form; the GM chooses. Observed on Cast a Spell and Brew a Potion. Expected: a player choice control before the next GM turn, with the chosen cost applied to state.
- **Academia Arcana: active conditions are not shown on the Character tab (medium).** Burnout was recorded (`condition.added burnout`, Stress 2), but the sheet has no Conditions line. `scene.sheet` is built from `_sheet_text(...)` in `src/cygnusloom/services/views.py`; `_status_lines` (which includes `Conditions:`) is only used for the GM statblock. Expected: show active conditions and their modifiers on the player sheet.
- **Academia Arcana: no rest/advance_time/market tools (medium).** Conditions end on `rest`/`downtime`, but AA has no rest or time-advance tool; clearing Burnout depends entirely on the GM calling `end_condition`. AA also has no item catalogue/market. Expected: generic rest/time hooks or an AA downtime flow, and an explicit economy path.
- **UI: advancement radio buttons all look selected (low).** All four options render as filled white dots even with `checked=false`. The global `input` rule in `src/cygnusloom/web/static/style.css:70` applies background/border/padding to radios, and the advancement page has no reset. Expected: native radio appearance or a checked-only style.
- **UI: "Randomize all" leaves the required name blank (low).** `randomizeAll` in `src/cygnusloom/web/static/creation.js` does not fill `input[name="name"]`; for Academia (no name pool) the required field blocks submit. Cairn only filled it because a background name pool supplied a name. Expected: fill a name or relabel the button.
- **Tool ergonomics: ids vs names and map placement (low).** Recoverable but repeated: `move_actor` rejected `"isthmus-town"` ("there is no location named ...") when the GM used the world id; `place_map_token`/`place_participant` rejected blocked cells; duplicate map/location/NPC names produced errors. Each costs a GM round. Expected: accept ids as aliases, expose map occupancy/dimensions in tool results, and make duplicate-name errors point at the existing object.
- **Playtest note: DeepSeek malformed tool calls (low).** Recoverable model noise: invalid JSON arguments, `quirks` as a string, unknown arguments (`stat` on `player_save`, `background`), invalid icon `lab`, nonexistent tool `find_nothing`. The engine returned clear errors and the GM recovered; consider schema/prompt hardening if this recurs.

Verified working in the same run: WWN level-up (HP/saves/attack bonus/skills, and Alert correctly raised Notice to 2), Cairn encounter initiative/player damage/End turn/Start NPC turn, AA Stress→Burnout and College Cup reset, with no 5xx responses or `turn.failed` events. One initial CSRF/login failure in a parallel session was a test-harness cookie collision across ports, not an app bug.
